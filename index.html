Know More

I am a PhD Scholar in the Department of Electrical, Electronics and Communication Engineering at IIT Dharwad.

My research interests include VLSI systems, AI hardware accelerators, ASIC design methodologies, energy-efficient computing architectures, and hardware-software co-design for machine learning systems.

I completed my M.Tech in VLSI Design and Embedded Systems and B.E in Electronics and Communication Engineering with strong academic and practical exposure to semiconductor systems and digital hardware design.
